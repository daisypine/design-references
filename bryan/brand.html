02Typography

BBH Bogle, Regular (400), uppercase, 0% tracking. Carries every headline, the logo wordmark, the stat numbers, and button labels. This is the voice of the brand - chunky and confident, used with restraint.
BodyInstrument Sans
Instrument Sans, Regular (400), Title Case, 20px / 30px line height. Paragraphs, subheads, and card copy. Calm and readable - never cramped, never competing with the display face.

05Do & Don't

Do

  • Use red like a brushstroke - decisive and occasional
  • Let wood and dark carry the warmth
  • Keep generous margins and quiet type
  • Reserve the italic for Bryan's real quotes

Don't

  • Flood the page with red or use it for body text
  • Add clichéd Japanese decoration (torii, blossoms, brush fonts)
  • Crowd the layout or box everything in
  • Mix in a second accent color
