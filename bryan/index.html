About Bryan

The calls I have all day long — that's what the world needs to see.

Bryan Russell has been in mortgage lending for 28 years. Not doing the same deal over and over — running a brokerage of 200+ loan officers, solving everyone else's complicated cases on top of his own. That compounding experience is what separates him.

He doesn't wear a suit. He doesn't follow a script. He backs up before the paperwork and builds a real plan — for the first-time buyer who needs a roadmap and the multimillion-dollar investor who needs the deal structured right.

What Makes Bryan Different

Most lenders see your deal.
Bryan sees your situation.

Twenty-eight years — including thousands of complex cases solved for 200+ loan officers he oversaw as broker of record. He's seen nearly every scenario. The hard ones are the interesting ones.

01
He doesn't start with the paperwork

The first conversation isn't about documents. It's about your client's plan — how long they'll be in the property, where they want to be in ten years. That kind of discovery doesn't happen in most mortgage offices. It happens here.

02
He's worked the hard ones

Running a brokerage of 200+ loan officers means Bryan didn't just close his own transactions — he solved everyone else's complicated ones too. The exceptions, the edge cases, the deals with "hair on them." That experience compounds.

03
He talks directly to decision-makers

When a deal needs an exception, Bryan doesn't escalate to a manager and wait. He calls the head of underwriting at the lender — because he knows how to position a deal from the risk side, not just the borrower side.

04
He knows why, not just what

Most loan officers follow the checklist because someone told them to. Bryan follows it because he understands every condition, every requirement, and when to push back. If an underwriter's condition doesn't hold up, he'll say so — and usually wins.
