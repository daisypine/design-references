Our Story

How a Bicycle Built One of Redwood City's Most Trusted Landscape Crews

In 2009, JDH Garden Services started with nothing more than a bicycle, a handful of tools, and a simple promise: to work hard and treat every single yard with absolute care. Our founder, Jose, rode from house to house in Redwood City, earning the trust of his neighbors one lawn at a time.

Today, we've traded the bicycle for a fleet of fully equipped trucks and a professional crew - but our core values haven't changed an inch. We are still a family-owned, local business. We don't cut corners, we don't skip the details, and we still show up with the same grit and work ethic that started it all.

Everything to Know Before We Get Started

Why hire a professional for lawn care instead of doing it myself? +
A healthy lawn takes more than a mow. Our crew reads your soil, dials in the right cutting height and feeding schedule, and heads off weeds, pests, and disease before they spread and get expensive. You get your weekends back, and your lawn stays green, thick, and healthy year round.
Do you design custom landscapes, or just maintain them? +
Both. We design outdoor spaces around how you actually want to use them, balancing looks, function, and low upkeep. From plant choices to walkways, patios, and water features, we factor in your soil, sun, and drainage so everything thrives. Whether it's a full redesign or a few upgrades, we build it to last.
How do I know if my irrigation system needs repair? +
Watch for uneven watering, dry or soggy patches, weak pressure, pooling around sprinkler heads, or heads that won't pop up. A creeping water bill usually points to a hidden leak. If your plants are wilting or you're seeing brown spots, that's your cue to have it checked before a small issue turns into a costly one.
Do you take on commercial properties? +
Yes. We handle landscaping and irrigation for offices, retail centers, and other commercial sites, keeping them clean, welcoming, and easy to maintain. We build in water efficiency and keep you compliant with local rules, so your property looks sharp for clients and staff without any surprises.
Do you offer free estimates? +
Always. Tell us a bit about your property and Jose's team will get back to you within 24 hours to set one up. No cost, no pressure, just a straight answer on what your yard needs.
