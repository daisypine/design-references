The Vein Center

The reason most patients find us.

Heaviness, aching, swelling by the end of the day, veins you've stopped wearing shorts to hide. These are medical problems, not cosmetic ones. Dr. Shakir maps the vein with ultrasound first, then chooses the treatment that fits your anatomy.

  • VenaSeal™Medical adhesive closes the vein. No heat, no tumescent anesthesia, no compression stockings.
  • ClosureFast™Radiofrequency ablation for larger diseased veins and chronic venous insufficiency.
  • SclerotherapyAn injected solution collapses spider and smaller varicose veins.
  • VeinGogh™Thermolysis for the fine vessels too small to inject, under 1 mm.
  • Excel V™ Laser[CONFIRM] Facial veins, broken capillaries and vascular lesions.

PDO Thread Lift

Lift, without the surgery.

Dissolvable polydioxanone threads are placed under the skin to lift the brow, cheeks, jawline or neck, and to stimulate your own collagen as they absorb over the following months. Most patients are back to normal within a few days.
