Our story

Same family. Same phone number. Since 1938.

Motor Body Company started towing San Jose in 1938. Norm and Vince Alongi opened their own shop ten years later, and by the 1970s the two had become one business — the name over the door changed, the trucks never stopped rolling.

Today it's Nick and Russell running it, the third generation of the same family. Their father Stan spent his career on these roads and helped push California's "slow down or move over" law into existence — the one that protects every tow operator standing on a shoulder at night.

That's the whole idea here: a family business, in one city, doing one thing well, for a very long time.

  • 1st Norm & Vince Alongi
    Started the business and put the first trucks on the road.
  • 2nd Stan & Mark Alongi
    Grew the fleet and fought for the laws that keep tow operators alive.
  • 3rd Nick & Russell Alongi
    Running it today, out of the same shop on Sunol Street.
