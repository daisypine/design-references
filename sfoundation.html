sFoundation — home page wireframe & direction

Mid-fidelity wireframe for a custom redesign of the current sfoundation.io (outsourced finance & accounting for small & medium businesses). Keep the brand green but elevate it — the current site is a flat corporate green; this direction pushes it premium and editorial. Content below is pulled from the live site. Colors and type are the direction; photos and final logo are open.

Concept

Built on a solid foundation. The name is the idea: sFoundation is the financial foundation a business stands on. Three service tiers stack from the ground up — Bookkeeping (the groundwork), Controller (the structure), CFO (the vantage point where you steer). The signature scroll section makes it literal.

Palette — warm paper & sage base, greens as the structure, brass as the accent

#F5F7F1 paper
#E6ECDD sage
#0F3327 pine
#2E8B62 green
#B08A3E brass

Type

Open to any fonts. Shown in Fraunces (serif display, warm + editorial for trust) + Hanken Grotesk (clean sans) as a starting point — swap freely to fit the final brand. The serif is what pulls it out of "flat corporate green" territory.

Logo

Keeping the "sFoundation" wordmark. Working mark here is a small stacked/layered glyph (three tiers on a base line) to echo the foundation concept — final mark is open.

Signature interaction — the foundation build

A scroll-triggered section where a structure builds tier by tier from the ground up. The facade is a grid of ledger cells — the business is literally built out of clean books. Demo below shows the idea.

Photography

Stock / placeholders for now. Wants a human touch — real founders and business owners, and the sFoundation team at work. Avoid generic "handshake / stock finance" clichés. Placeholders marked in dashed boxes.

Reference (what they liked)

  • A layered scroll interaction from a previous build — plus an editorial-clean layout, one clear CTA, homepage FAQ, and a trust/results row. This wireframe carries those patterns over to sFoundation.

Scope note

This is the home page only, for direction + the signature feature. Interior pages (CFO Services, Bookkeeping, About, Resources) would follow the same system once the home is approved.

Section tags (brass pills, top-right of each block) note what each section is doing. Scroll down to walk the page.
