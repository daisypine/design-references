How it started

“I said, why is this so difficult — and why has there only been one flavor for a hundred years?”
R
Rodni
Founder · St. George, UT

It was a night at home with the family, fireplace going, somebody said let's make s'mores. Then came the balancing act — marshmallow on the chocolate, cracker on top, the whole thing tipping over.

So he wrote his own marshmallow recipe and put the ingredients inside it. Chocolate chips in the center, so it melts from the inside out and there's nothing to balance on the cracker.

One flavor turned into six. Chocolate peanut butter. Milk chocolate. Chocolate chip. Mint. Maple walnut. A coffee cappuccino with a real kick of caffeine.

Then came the name. He'd just made more flavors — so, S'more Flavors. Still a family operation, still made in small batches in southern Utah.

How to make one

Three steps. No balancing.

STEP 01

Pick your flavor

Straight from the bag. The filling is already in there, so there's nothing to unwrap, break, or stack.

STEP 02

Toast it

Campfire, fireplace, gas burner, broiler. Golden to nearly black — the center goes molten either way.

STEP 03

Press between crackers

It sits flat and stays put. Two grahams, one squeeze, done. Hand it to a kid without a napkin standing by.
